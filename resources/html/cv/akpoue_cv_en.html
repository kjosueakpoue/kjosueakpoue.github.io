🎯 Profile Summary

Doctoral Researcher in Linguistics specializing in formal semantics, with a primary focus on semantic composition and decomposition (at the word, phrase, sentence, and discourse levels). Experienced in testing theoretical semantic models against empirical, primary data from understudied languages (including Ivorian French, Baoulé, and other Kwa and Ivorian languages). Combines rigorous fieldwork methods with practical, self-taught engineering skills in formal language design (custom DSLs, human-oriented markup syntax) and open science architectures. Equipped to contribute immediately to cutting-edge research and innovative teaching in theoretical semantics and digital humanities.

🚀 Key Highlights
  • From Field Data to Formal Theory: Rare dual expertise bridging empirical language documentation and field methodology with formal semantic testing on understudied non-Indo-European languages.
  • Applied Formal Languages & Tool Design: Pragmatic designer of human-oriented, machine-readable specifications—creator of custom markup languages (WRIML), annotation/archiving systems (WRIDAL, WRIMLIFT), ASCII phonetic encodings (WRASCI), and scientific DSLs (phi).
  • Open Science & Infrastructure Advocate: Founder of CILARCO (Côte d'Ivoire Linguistique Archive Ouverte), applying FAIR data principles, Git workflows, and Zenodo integration for sustainable African language preservation.

🔬 Research Interests
  • Formal Semantics: Semantic compositionality and decompositionality (analyzing how sub-lexical and complex units assemble meaning across words, phrases, sentences, and discourse).
  • Empirical & Typological Testing: Testing, refining, and extending formal semantic models using empirical data from understudied language structures (Kwa, Ivorian languages, Ivorian French).
  • Linguistic Data Architecture: Design principles for human-centric, structured formats for primary linguistic data annotation and preservation.

🛠️ Software Standards & Language Projects
WRIML
WRIting Markup Language
A novel, human-oriented markup language designed to match XML in rigor and extensibility while maximizing readability and authoring speed.
Annotation
WRIDAL & WRIMLIFT
Specialized tools and specifications engineered for efficient annotation, structured processing, and long-term archiving of primary linguistic data.
WRASCI
WRiting ASCii Ipa
An ASCII-input encoding standard mapping International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) characters using strict geometric suffix rules (inversion, reversal, rounding, barring).
The phi Project
Scientific DSL
A domain-specific language (DSL) tailored for scientific writing and automated multi-platform LaTeX compilation.
