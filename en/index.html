Research

Empirically grounded theoretical research on linguistic meaning.

Axis I — Theoretical

Neo-componential semantics & theory of ambiguity

How should the modularity of linguistic meaning be encoded in a formal, compositional framework? And what does it mean for a theory of meaning to take ambiguity seriously as a structural property of language — not a residue to be explained away?

Axis II — Thematic

Plurality, quantification, coordination, nominal semantics

Ivorian languages — particularly Kwa languages — as a primary empirical ground for challenging and refining formal models developed mostly on the basis of well-studied European languages.
